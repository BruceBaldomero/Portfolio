About Me

Hi, I'm Bruce — a London-based Technical Operations & QA Specialist at Adaro Optics, where I keep systems running, issues resolved, and workflows improving for clients like Vision Express, David Clulow, Boots, Specsavers, and independent practices across the UK.

My work spans SQL scripting, Playwright test automation, UAT, regression testing, charge run monitoring, database and dashboard management, SLA-aligned incident handling, and cross-team technical support.

I thrive in fast-paced environments, solving complex problems and building solutions that actually stick. Open to remote and office-based roles.

Outside of work: bouldering, cycling, football, and spending time with family and friends.
